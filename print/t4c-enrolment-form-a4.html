Section 7 · What you are agreeing to

The school day. Live classes run Monday to Thursday. Learners attend four lessons a day, four hours a day, on camera with a live teacher. Extra Skills Fridays are part of the programme and attendance is expected.

Attendance and conduct. Learners join lessons on time with the camera on. Repeated absence or conduct that disrupts a class of sixteen is addressed with the guardian first, and may lead to suspension of the place.

Fees. Tuition is invoiced monthly or annually in the currency chosen on page 1. One calendar month's written notice is required to withdraw a learner; the annual registration and application fee is not refundable once a place has been reserved.
