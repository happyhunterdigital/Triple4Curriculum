I am pleased to confirm that we would be delighted to welcome your child to Triple 4 Curriculum, and this letter sets out what the first term will look like, what you will need to send us, and the date by which we would like everything in.

Our school day runs from Monday to Thursday. Four lessons a day, four hours of live teaching in focused blocks, and a three day weekend that belongs to your family. Classes are capped at sixteen learners, and every lesson is led live by a qualified teacher on camera. Parents step back: once Foundation learners are logged in, the teacher owns the room.

On the fifth day we run Extra Skills Fridays, where learners work through real projects in coding, finance, arts and leadership. It is the part of the week our learners talk about most.
