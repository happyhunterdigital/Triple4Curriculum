# Launch content pack: introducing Triple 4 Curriculum

Seven posts that introduce the school across Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn
and TikTok. Copy is written for each platform, not cross-posted verbatim.
Handles, facts and links match `docs/social-launch.md`, which remains the
source of truth.

---

## 1. Guardrails

- **Voice:** empowering, caring, inspiring, professional. Warm, plain
  sentences. Short paragraphs. No exclamation marks, no hype, no em or en
  dashes.
- **Never claim:** guaranteed results, pass rates, "best school", unverified
  rankings, or a fee that is not on the pricing page.
- **Pupil privacy:** no learner faces, names, or work without written guardian
  consent (POPIA and the Children's Act). Use hands, backs of heads, screens,
  classroom stills, or the crest and brand tiles instead.
- **Accessibility:** every image carries alt text (list in section 8). Video
  carries burned-in captions and a spoken URL at the end.
- **Links:** every post uses the admissions URL with the platform's UTM. Never
  the bare homepage.
